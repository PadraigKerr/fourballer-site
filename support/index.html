Getting help

Fourballer records golf scores and works out who owes whom at the end of a round. It does not move money, hold balances or take payments.

For help with the app, a problem with a round, or a question about your data, email support@fourballer.com.

Your account and your data

You can delete your account from inside the app, under Profile → Delete account. Rounds you played with other people stay in their history, with your name shown as “Former player”, because a round and any money settled on it are a shared record. Your name, phone number, email address and sign-in details are deleted.

The privacy policy sets out what is kept and for how long.
